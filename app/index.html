Setting it up

  1. Fix this phone so it sees the whole cage floor, and plug it in. It must not move afterwards.
  2. Start the camera. With him out of the cage, take the empty-cage picture.
  3. Put him back. A box should follow him, and stay on him when he lies still.
  4. Retake the picture whenever the phone, the cage or his bedding moves.

The pictures stay on this phone; nothing is uploaded. The camera works only while this screen is open.
